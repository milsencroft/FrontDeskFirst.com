Why speed matters

A patient who does not hear back quickly may book with another clinic. Replying first, clearly, and making booking easy gives you the best chance of seeing them. Slow replies are common: in a 2026 test of 114 B2B companies, only 1 sent a personalised email within 5 minutes (Workato).

Your front desk is busy with patients in the chair. FrontDesk First handles the first reply and the follow-ups so no enquiry is forgotten.

How it works

  1. Enquiry arrives from your website form, WhatsApp button or email.
  2. Instant reply in your clinic's voice, using answers you approve in advance.
  3. Quick questions by tap-to-answer buttons: treatment, urgency, preferred time.
  4. Booking link to your calendar, so patients choose a slot themselves.
  5. Follow-ups until they book or opt out, and a daily summary for your team.

We never give medical advice. Anything outside your approved answers goes straight to your staff.
