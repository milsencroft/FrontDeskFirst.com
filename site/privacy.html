What we collect

From clinics and prospects: business name, contact name, business email and phone, website, and messages you send us. From patients of our client clinics (on the clinic's behalf): name, contact details, enquiry message, treatment interest and preferred appointment time.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data, buy contact lists, or use patient data for our own marketing. We do not collect medical records.

Who we share it with

Service providers that run our service: Cloudflare (hosting and database), Google (email), Meta (WhatsApp messages for clinics), Cal.com (booking), Razorpay (payments). Each processes data only to provide its service.

How long we keep it

Prospect data: until you opt out or 12 months after last contact. Patient enquiry data: as long as the clinic remains our client, then deleted within 30 days unless the clinic asks us to return it.

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, by emailing hello@frontdeskfirst.com. We reply within 30 days. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other laws that apply.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is restricted to the people who need it, and each clinic's data is kept separate.
