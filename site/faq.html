Questions clinics ask

Do I pay before I see it working?

No. We set it up on your own enquiries first. After 48 hours of seeing patients get answered, you pay the setup fee and first month, or prepay 6 months and get 1 month free. If you're not happy, you don't pay and we switch it off.

Will it give medical advice?

No. It only sends replies you approve. Anything else, including emergencies, goes straight to your staff.

Do I need to change my software or WhatsApp number?

Usually not. We add a form or WhatsApp button to your website and connect your calendar. WhatsApp automation runs on your clinic's own WhatsApp Business account; we guide you through Meta's setup.

Who pays for WhatsApp messages?

Meta bills your clinic's account directly for any paid messages. Replies to patients who message you first are generally free under Meta's current pricing.

What about patient privacy?

We collect only what's needed to reply and book, keep each clinic's data separate, never sell data, and delete it within 30 days if you leave. See our privacy policy.

Will patients mind automated replies?

Replies are written in your clinic's voice and are short and helpful. Patients can always reach your team, and can opt out of follow-ups by replying STOP.

Do you guarantee more patients?

No. We make sure every enquiry gets a fast, consistent reply and follow-up. Results depend on your clinic, offer and market.

Can I cancel?

Yes, with 30 days' notice. Unused prepaid months are refunded pro rata.

How do I talk to you?

By email at hello@frontdeskfirst.com or the contact form. We reply within one business day, and we can send a short personalised video for your clinic.
